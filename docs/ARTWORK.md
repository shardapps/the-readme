# Artwork

The [README banner](../assets/readme-banner.jpg), welcome illustration in [cover.jpg](../assets/cover.jpg), and app icon in [readme-icon.png](../assets/readme-icon.png) were generated with OpenAI ImageGen. The app and installer use the multiresolution [readme.ico](../assets/readme.ico). Screenshots in the README come from the running WPF UI.

## README banner prompt

> Make a premium GitHub repository header image for a real Windows desktop application by Shard Apps. Very wide panoramic composition, approximately 3.2:1. Refined editorial product branding. Background deep indigo, near-black plum, with a subtle tiny dotted grid and a faint cool blue glow on the far right. On the right half, a sculptural clean lilac sheet of paper with a geometric folded jewel-violet corner, slightly stacked on two sheets, lit with a restrained faint mint rim. Behind that stack on far right, a thin translucent dark glass panel with a few small abstract color-coded source-code lines and geometric brackets. On the left, typeset exactly two lines with perfect spelling: 'The Readme' in large warm white modern sans, and 'Markdown editor for Windows' underneath in smaller muted lilac. Large open margins. Keep all subjects and text centered vertically in the middle 56% of the image so it can crop at top and bottom into an ultrawide GitHub banner. No logo, no watermark, no other words or labels, no decorative badges, no fake buttons. Deliberate, understated and visually sharp.

## Welcome illustration prompt

> Wide 3:2 editorial product illustration, for a Windows Markdown reader called The Readme. No text, no logos, no typography anywhere. Refined dark indigo and aubergine background, luminous pale lilac folded sheets of paper stacked at a graceful diagonal with softly bevelled edges, a jewel violet translucent folded corner, fine chartreuse edge light, soft shadows and subtle grid of tiny low contrast dots. High-end editorial 3D product photography, clean geometry, restrained and calm. Keep focal sculptural subject centered, with generous negative space at all edges. No UI mockup, no lettering, no stars or busy decoration. Intended as the artwork inside a reading app and its GitHub README.

## App icon prompt

> A single centered premium Windows app icon on transparent background. At 16px it must read as a clean lilac document with a deep-violet folded upper right corner, two dark-purple bold short horizontal lines on the lower left like lines of a document. Slightly rounded corners and one subtle dark purple secondary sheet behind. Near-flat front view, high contrast, simple geometric silhouette, soft lavender fill, violet shadow and a tiny mint reflected highlight. Fill 82% of the square. No app tile, no framing device, no backdrop, no real text or letters, no glow beyond icon, no words.
