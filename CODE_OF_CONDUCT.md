# Code of conduct

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Treat people with respect, welcome different levels of experience, and keep disagreements focused on the work. Respect boundaries and protect private information.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing private information without consent are grounds for moderation.

Use GitHub’s report feature on the relevant comment, issue, or pull request, or contact a Shard Apps maintainer privately when you can. If a maintainer is involved, report the behavior directly to GitHub. Provide links and enough context for a moderator to investigate.

Maintainers may remove content, warn contributors, restrict interactions, or block accounts. They will use the least extensive action appropriate to the circumstances and handle reports discreetly.
