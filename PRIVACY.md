# Privacy

The Readme by Shard Apps reads documents on your device. It sends no document content, file paths, diagnostics, or analytics to Shard Apps. The app does not need an account.

Preferences are stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ShardApps\TheReadme\preferences.json`. By default, they include up to eight recent file paths and your theme, zoom, and sidebar choices. Clear the recent list or disable it in Settings. To remove preferences after uninstalling, delete that file.

Remote images are never fetched by the app. If you follow an external link, Windows opens it in the relevant handler, such as your browser or mail client. That application and external destination have their own privacy practices. Files and links you choose can also be subject to your own system, network, and file-provider settings.
